Set up

One step needs you to switch the Skylight's power off and on at its light switch or breaker. Use a phone or a portable computer for setup, so you can take it with you.

  1. Prepare your Bluetooth proxy

    Add the Skylight firmware to the ESPHome Bluetooth proxy nearest the panel.

    Now install it: in ESPHome Device Builder, open , then choose ⋮ → Install → Wirelessly. This page continues by itself when the proxy is back.

    Open ESPHome Device Builder

  2. Connect Home Assistant to the proxy

  3. Let Home Assistant hear your remote

    This lets Home Assistant follow the presses on your own remote.

  4. Pair Home Assistant with the panel

    1. Switch the panel's power off at its light switch or breaker, then on again. The remote's power button does not work for this.
    2. Wait until the panel is back on. That can take up to 30 seconds.
    3. Select Pair straight away. The panel blinks.

    Now test it.

    It works. Now choose a mode. The panel switches on in that mode.

Start over

Home Assistant is paired. Pairing it again can make the panel drop your remote, so start over instead. This removes every remote, Home Assistant included, and then pairs them again in the right order.

  1. Switch the panel's power off at its light switch or breaker, then on again.
  2. Wait until the panel is back on. Then select Unpair all straight away. The panel blinks. If it only switches off, the request came too late. Try again.
  3. Pair your remote: switch the power off and on, wait until the panel is back on, then hold the remote's power button for 10 seconds until the panel blinks.
  4. Select Continue. The setup checklist pairs Home Assistant.

Unpair all remotes

This makes the panel forget every remote, Home Assistant included. Use Start over above. It unpairs everything and pairs your remote and Home Assistant again.

The panel ignores Home Assistant after a backup restore

An older backup has an older command counter, and the panel ignores old numbers. Moving the counter forward fixes this. It sends nothing to the panel.

Install the firmware by hand

Download the Skylight firmware package. Put skylight-transmitter.yaml and the components folder next to your proxy's YAML, then add:

packages:
  skylight: !include skylight-transmitter.yaml
